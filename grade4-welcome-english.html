الدرس الرابع: Adjectives in Context & Classmate Talk (Page 7)

📖 من كتاب الطالب (Student's Book - Page 7 - Activities 5 & 6)

استخدام الصفات في سياق الجمل الواقعية، والتدرب على المحادثة والسؤال مع الزميل.

📝 الصفات في جمل من واقع الحياة (Read and write. Then look and match):

1. My little brother is very naughty. (أخي الصغير مشاغب جداً) 😈 🔊
2. I don't like the taste of cheese. It's terrible. (لا أحب طعم الجبنة، إنه فظيع) 🧀 🔊
3. I got ten presents from my friends! I was happy and surprised. (حصلت على ١٠ هدايا! كنت سعيداً ومتفاجئاً) 🎁 🔊
4. There was a shark near me when I was in the water. I was afraid. (كان هناك سمكة قرش بالقرب مني، كنت خائفاً) 🦈 🔊
5. When I go on vacation, I think it's exciting. (عندما أذهب في إجازة، أعتقد أنها مثيرة وحماسية) 🏖️ 🔊

👥 محادثة صفيّة: إسأل زميلك (Talk with a classmate. Ask and answer):

👦
Q: "What are you afraid of?" ➔ A: "I'm afraid of tigers." 🔊
مما تخاف؟ ➔ أخاف من النمور.
👧
Q: "What is exciting at school?" ➔ A: "Playing games with my friends is exciting at school." 🔊
ما المثير في المدرسة؟ ➔ لعب الألعاب مع أصدقائي شيء مثير في المدرسة.
👦
Q: "Who is naughty in your family?" ➔ A: "My little brother." 🔊
من هو المشاغب في عائلتك؟ ➔ أخي الصغير.
👧
Q: "What makes you surprised?" ➔ A: "Presents from my parents and friends!" 🔊
ما الذي يفاجئك؟ ➔ هدايا والدي وأصدقائي تفاجئني!

الدرس السابع: Past Simple with Was / Were (Page 9)

📖 من كتاب الطالب (Student's Book - Page 9 - Activities 5 & 6)

زمن الماضي البسيط باستخدام (was / wasn't / were / weren't) وقراءة نص قصة احتفال يوم الأحد والإجابة عنها.

📌 قاعدة استخدام الفعل في الماضي (Past Simple Be):

• was / wasn't: للمفرد (I, He, She, It, Sunday, food, chocolate cake).

• were / weren't: للجمع (We, They, You, musicians, sandwiches, parents).

🍰 نص احتفال المدرسة (The Back to School Celebration):

Sunday 1. was the back to school celebration day! We 2. were happy at the celebration because it 3. was fun. The celebration 4. wasn't in the classroom, it 5. was on the school playground. There 6. were three young musicians, and the food 7. was delicious! There 8. were sandwiches, and there 9. was a big chocolate cake, too. All the All Sorts and our teacher, were there. Our parents 10. weren't at the celebration because they 11. were at work. It 12. was the best day of school!

❓ أسئلة الفهم والاستيعاب (Read again and answer):

1. Where were the students and the All Sorts on Sunday?
➔ They were at the school celebration (on the playground).
2. Was the celebration in the classroom?
➔ No, it wasn't. (It was on the school playground).
3. What was delicious?
➔ The food and the chocolate cake were delicious!
4. Who wasn't at the celebration?
➔ Their parents weren't at the celebration because they were at work.
5. Was the celebration the worst day at school?
➔ No, it wasn't. (It was the best day of school!).
