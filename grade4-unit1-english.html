📖 اقرأ واملاً الفراغات بالصفة المناسبة (Look, read and answer)

اختر الكلمة المناسبة من الصندوق لملء الفراغات، واضغط على الجملة للاستماع لنطقها:

best better biggest longer more difficult nicest smaller taller tallest worst

In the first photo, you can see my (1) friend at school. Her name is Asma. She has (2) hair than me, and she is (3) , too. I think she is the (4) girl in our class.

We always sit next to each other. She is brilliant! She is the (5) person I know. Asma helps her friends and smiles a lot.

The second photo is of our class with the teacher. Our class is the (6) in our school. There are 30 students!

The other classes are (7) with about 20 students in each one. In this photo, we are in our math class. I like the school subjects we study, but math is (8) than geography or history.

Sometimes I don't understand math, and I think I am the (9) student in class! But Asma always helps me because she is (10) at math than me.

الدرس السادس: Grammar - How often...?

📖 من كتاب الطالب (We Can 3 - Page 12-13)

السؤال بـ "How often" للحديث عن تكرار الأفعال والأنشطة مع أفراد العائلة (How often do you / does she...?).

🧠 اختبار قبلي (Pre-Test) - ٣ أسئلة

١. أكمل السؤال: "How often ______ your aunt visit?"

٢. "How often do you play football?"

٣. استمع للكلمة وحدد معناها: "visit" 🔊 استمع

👦
How often do you see your cousins? 🔊
كم مرة ترى فيها أبناء عمك؟
👧
I always see them on Fridays at my grandparents' house! 🔊
أراهم دائماً في أيام الجمعة في منزل جدي وجدتي!
💡 أسئلة أثناء الشرح (During-Lesson Questions) - سؤالين

١. "How often ______ they help their mother?"

٢. "She ______ washes the dishes." (100% frequency)

🎓 اختبار بعدي (Post-Test) - ٤ أسئلة

١. "How often ______ your father play tennis?"

٢. ما هي التهجئة (Spelling) الصحيحة للكلمة "مرة واحدة"؟

٣. "I ______ eat fast food because it is unhealthy." (0% frequency)

٤. ما هو الرمز الذي يعبر عن "كم مرة" (How often / clock)?
