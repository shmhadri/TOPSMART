Lesson 3: Grammar in Action (القواعد: How often & ظروف التكرار - ص ١٢-١٣)

📖 مرجع كتاب الطالب (Student's Book - Pages 12 & 13 - Grammar)

تعلم كيفية السؤال عن معدل تكرار الأنشطة باستخدام How often، واستخدام ظروف التكرار الخمسة (always 100%, usually 75%, sometimes 40%, rarely 10%, never 0%)، مع تمارين الترتيب والخيارات التفاعلية.

🧠 ١. التقييم القبلي التشخيصي (Pre-Assessment - Warm-up)

"How often do you walk in the park?" ➔ "I ________ walk in the morning." (100% دائماً)

💬 محادثات كتاب الطالب التفاعلية (Listen & Practice):
🚶‍♂️
Q: "How often do you walk in the park?"
A: "I always walk in the morning." / "I usually walk after school." 🔊
كم اعتدت على المشي في المنتزه؟ ➔ دائماً أمشي في الصباح / غالباً بعد المدرسة.
🐰
Q: "How often do you feed Daisy? Does she eat every day?"
A: "I never forget to feed her." 🔊
كم اعتدت على إطعام ديزي؟ هل تأكل يومياً؟ ➔ لم أنسَ إطعامها قط!
🐱
Q: "How often does it happen with cats?"
A: "Achoo! It happens sometimes." / "Very rarely. Maybe I have a cold." 🔊
كيف يحدث ذلك مع القطط؟ ➔ يعطس! عفو، يحدث ذلك أحياناً / نادراً جداً.
📝 ٢. اقرأ وأجب (Activity 2: Read and answer - Page 12):

1. What phrase do we use to ask about the frequency of activities?

2. Which words are used to express frequency?

3. Which word is used to talk about activities we do all the time (100%)?

4. Which word is used to talk about activities we do not do (0%)?

5. Which words are used to talk about activities we do less often?

🔘 ٣. اختر الكلمات الصحيحة (Activity 3: Read and choose - Page 13):

1. How often (do / does) you see your cousins? ➔ I (always / rarely) see them. We go to school together every day.

2. How often (do / does) your aunt give you presents? ➔ My aunt (always / never) gives me beautiful presents when we meet.

3. How often (do / does) you visit your grandparents? ➔ I (rarely / always) visit them. They live in another country.

4. How often (do / does) your parents take your cat to the vet? ➔ They (usually / never) take him when he needs vaccines.

5. How often (do / does) your father have dinner with your uncle? ➔ They (always / never) have dinner together. My uncle loves my dad's burgers.

لمن كان هذا المنزل؟ لم يكن منزلنا القديم، بل كان منزل أجدادنا (ملكهم).
