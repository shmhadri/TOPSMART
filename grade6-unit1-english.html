🌳 Fun in the Park (حديقة المرح) - Track 1.5

استمع للقصة كاملة مع تمييز الحوارات بين أولي وآمبر والأم:

Oli and his younger sister, Amber, are sent on a walk in the park by their mom. Amber is excited as she is tested by her brother about the different creatures in the park. She is learning about creatures in school.

👦 Oli: "Look!" (pointing at the water) "There's a dinosaur!"
👧 Amber: "That's not a dinosaur!" (laughing) "They can't be seen anymore. They're extinct!"
👦 Oli: "You're right! It must be a butterfly," (scratching his head) "Look, can you see its wings?"
👧 Amber: "Nooo! It doesn't have wings! It's a tortoise!"

👦 Oli: "Oh, I see! Look over here on this plant. A beetle! You know some beetles are eaten by people!"
👧 Amber: "That's silly!" (as Oli pretends to put it in his mouth) "Stop! You can't eat it! It will move around in your mouth!"

👦 Oli: (holds Amber's arm, whispers) "Be quiet! I think that's a tiger. Careful! We don't want to be eaten!"
👧 Amber: "That's not right! Tigers aren't found in the park! That's just a cat!"
🐱 Suddenly, the cat meows at them.
👦 Oli: "Run!" (as they run out of the park, laughing all the way home).

👩 Mom: "What did you find in the park?"
👦 Oli: "All sorts of things!"
👧 Amber: "Yeah! We saw dinosaurs and tigers. And Oli ate a beetle!"
👩 Mom: "That sounds like fun!" (winking at Oli).

📊 النشاط 3: تصنيف الكائنات (Seen in the Park vs. Not Seen in the Park)

الكلمات: [ beetle, butterfly, dinosaur, cat, insects, tortoise, tiger ]

1. Seen in the Park (شوهد بالحديقة)
• beetle ➔ They saw a beetle near the water.
• ➔ They saw a tortoise in the grass.
• ➔ They saw a cat that meowed.
• ➔ They saw small insects on plants.
2. Not Seen in the Park (لم يُشاهد)
• ➔ Dinosaurs are extinct.
• ➔ Tigers aren't found in the park.

الدرس 7: معمل قواعد الجمل المكانية الموسع (Where Clauses - Page 17)

Where Clauses Master Lab

📖 النشاط 1: قواعد تكوين الجمل المكانية (Where Clauses Rules & Conversation)

• We can use where to refer to a place (نستخدم where للإشارة إلى المكان وما يحدث فيه).
• We use is to refer to one single place: "The desert is where camels live."
• We use are to refer to two or more places: "The trees are where monkeys play."
• We can use that or this instead of the place: "That's where birds lay eggs!"
• We move is or are to the beginning in questions: "Are those the trees where monkeys play?"
👦 Boy: "Are those the trees where the monkeys play?"
👩 Guide: "Yes! And look at that nest. That's where the birds lay their eggs!"

الدرس 8: قراءة بيئات الكائنات وتحويلات المبني للمجهول (Pages 18-19)

Reading & Writing Master

Different Animal Environments (بيئات الكائنات المختلفة) - Page 18

اقرأ واستمع للنص حول البيئات الثلاث:

🌊 Water-based (البيئة المائية)

Some animals, like the shark, octopus, or fish, live in the ocean. That's where the water is most salty and cold. These animals can breathe underwater! Many fish are eaten by sharks deep underwater. Others live in rivers, which is where many small fish are found, or in ponds and lakes. Swans don't breathe underwater, but they swim on top of it.

☀️ Desert-based (البيئة الصحراوية)

The desert is where the sun is really hot and there isn't much rain, so not all animals can have their home there. Camels, snakes, and tortoises all live in the desert. If you wanted to see them, you would have to put on strong sun protection and drink lots of water!

🌲 Forest-based (البيئة الغابية)

Forests are full of trees and offer lots of fresh food for the animals. You might find lots of different nests in the trees — that's where birds lay their eggs. The forest floor is where you might find lots of insects, as well as bigger animals, like tigers.

الدرس 9: العلوم الطبيعية وسمات الحيوانات (Natural Science: Animal Traits - Page 20)

Integrated Learning Master

Animal Traits (خصائص وسمات الحيوانات) - Page 20

تساعد هذه السمات الحيوانات على البقاء (Survival): أجزاء الجسم، أغطية الجسم، والسلوكيات.

🐪 🐧 1. Body Parts (أجزاء الجسم)

• Camels: The furry hump stores extra fat, allowing camels to live without drinking water for up to seven months!
• Penguins: Use their wings as flippers to swim swiftly through water to catch food.

🐻‍❄️ 🦎 2. Body Coverings (أغطية الجسم)

• Polar bears: Covered in thick white fur to stay warm in freezing winters and hide in snow.
• Chameleons: Constantly change their color and skin texture to hide from danger and communicate emotions.

🦝 🐙 3. Behavior (السلوكيات)

• Raccoons: Highly adaptable eaters that have learned to eat wasted food in urban trash cans.
